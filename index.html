01 The Destination

Lahore’s largest and most-visited retail destination.

Amanah Mall rises over Model Town Link Road — eight floors above ground and four below, welcoming around half a million visitors every month.

The lower four floors are given to shopping. Above them, the building turns to leisure: the food court, Magic Planet, the Velocity Fitness Club and a four-screen premium cinema. The newest additions include Pakistan’s largest trampoline park, an indoor amusement park and a VR park.

The mall is part of Amanah Group, an international real-estate business founded in 1995 with projects across the UK, UAE and Pakistan — built on commitment, excellence and long-term relationships.
